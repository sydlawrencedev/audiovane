Vibe your own

Say what you want.
An agent writes the shader.

Vibresia has a small server that AI assistants can talk to. Point your agent at it, describe a world in a sentence, and the agent writes the code, draws it, looks at the picture and tries again until it likes what it sees. Then it lands in your list and moves with your music.

How to do it

  1. Point your agent at Vibresia

    Vibresia ships a local server. Give your agent this and it can draw, check and save vibescapes:

    Connect to this mcp: vibresia -- "/Applications/Vibresia.app/Contents/MacOS/Vibresia" --mcp

    Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and the rest each want it spelled a little differently — the agents page has the version for yours.

  2. Ask for a world

    Describe it. The more specific you are about the movement, the better it comes out:

    Write a vibescape for Vibresia: a tunnel of liquid gold that pulses with the bass. Make it warm and slow.

  3. Watch it happen

    The agent draws the shader and looks at the picture, then changes it and draws it again. That loop is the whole trick: it does not describe what it wrote, it looks at it. When it is happy — or when you are — it saves, and the vibescape is in your list, moving with whatever is playing.

What the agent already knows

It can hear. Ask it what is playing and it gets the genre, the tempo and the shape of the spectrum, so it can write something that suits the music rather than something generic. It can also see every uniform a vibescape has, and preview a shader as a picture before it saves anything.

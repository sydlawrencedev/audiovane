# Minith Labs — Shareware Principles

## Our guiding principle

**Software should be worth sharing, not engineered to extract.**

Minith Labs makes software because useful, beautiful, curious or delightfully unnecessary things deserve to exist. We believe people should be able to discover software, try it freely and pass it on to others. If they love it, they can choose to support the people who made it.

AI is lowering the cost of creating software. We believe this opens the door to a new wave of independent software: small tools, personal projects, niche ideas and experiments made by people who care about them. We want to help bring back the generous spirit of the shareware era.

## What “proudly shareware” means

- **Free to use.** The core product is available without payment.
- **Donations are optional.** Users can support development if they want to. Payment must never be framed as an obligation.
- **Easy to discover and share.** Make it straightforward for people to try the software and recommend it to friends.
- **Built with care.** Free does not mean careless. The product should be useful, polished, reliable and actively maintained.
- **Respect the user.** No manipulative donation prompts, guilt, artificial urgency or dark patterns.
- **Be clear and honest.** Explain what the software does, what it costs (if anything), what data it uses and what users are allowed to do with it.
- **Make sharing legitimate.** State redistribution rights clearly. Encourage sharing within the boundaries of the licence.
- **Supporters deserve thanks, not pressure.** We can thank donors and recognise supporters, but donations should not secretly become a required subscription or gate to the essential experience.

## What this means for AudioVane

AudioVane is a music visualiser for Mac, created by Minith Labs.

Its promise is:

> **Proudly shareware. Free to use. Yours to share.**

AudioVane is free to use. People who enjoy it can choose to make a voluntary donation to support its development. There is no subscription and no pressure to pay.

The product should make it easy to:
- Understand what AudioVane does before downloading it.
- Download and use it without an unnecessary account or payment barrier.
- See a compelling demonstration of the visual experience.
- Share it with someone who loves music.
- Offer feedback or report a problem.
- Donate voluntarily, with a clear explanation of how support helps.

Do not claim that AudioVane is open source unless its source code and licence genuinely make it so. Shareware describes the distribution and payment philosophy; redistribution and modification rights must be defined separately and clearly.

## How we make decisions

When deciding on a feature, business model, launch tactic or marketing message, ask:

1. Does this make the software more useful, delightful or accessible?
2. Does it respect the user's freedom to try, use and share it?
3. Is the choice to donate genuinely voluntary?
4. Would we be comfortable explaining this decision publicly to our users?
5. Does it build long-term trust, even if it does not maximise short-term revenue?

If an idea depends on pressure, confusion, lock-in or making the free experience deliberately worse, it is probably not for us.

We still want to build sustainable products. Donations, sponsorship, paid extras or other models may be considered where they fit the product and are communicated honestly. Sustainability should support the mission, not quietly undermine it.

## The bigger ambition

AudioVane is one product. The principle extends across Minith Labs.

We want to make software that people recommend because they genuinely like it. We want to create things that might never have been built under a conventional venture-backed business model. We want AI to enable more independent creators to make and share useful software.

We are not trying to recreate every detail of the 1990s. We are bringing forward the best part of that era: the feeling of discovering a great little piece of software and telling someone else about it.

**AI is making software cheaper to create. Let's make great software easier to share.**

## Our short version

**Make useful, thoughtful software. Give it away freely. Invite support without pressure. Make sharing easy. Earn trust.**
